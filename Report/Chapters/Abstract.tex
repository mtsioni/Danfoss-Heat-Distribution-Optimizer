        \begin{abstract}
            \vspace{0.3cm}   
            Team of \acrshort{bsc} Software Engineering students developed an application that schedules production units focuses on cost efficiency. The product was effectuated on behalf of \acrfull{sdu} in collaboration with Danfoss.\vspace{0.3cm} 
              
            This application includes multiple aspects: the maintenance of the units, heating periods, two operational scenarios and graphical representation of the optimized schedule.\vspace{0.3cm} 
              
            This report describes the development process of the final product. Architecture followed \acrshort{solid} principles, \acrshort{mvvm} design pattern and was developed in C\# programming language. Management and communication were implemented through Scrum framework in Jira, while GitHub was the main remote repository host service.\vspace{0.3cm}
               
            Ultimately, reflection was conducted on the team's continuous improvement of technical and social skills, through challenges that occured during the development of this product. 
        \end{abstract}
